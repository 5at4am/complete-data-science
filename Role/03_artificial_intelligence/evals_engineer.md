# Evals Engineer

Designs how an organization actually knows its AI is good. Owns the measurement, benchmarks, and harnesses that tell teams whether models, RAG, and agents are improving or regressing.

## What They Do
- Design evaluation methodologies for LLM outputs
- Build eval harnesses and automated scoring pipelines
- Curate golden datasets and human-labeled rubrics
- Run regression testing on prompts, retrievers, and models
- Benchmark models before Q releases (pre-training, post-training)
- Track eval metrics in dashboards

## Key Skills
- LLM-as-judge patterns, human evaluation design
- Annotation workflows and rubrics
- Ragas, Braintrust, LangSmith, promptfoo
- Statistical rigor for comparing systems
- Python automation + CI/CD for evals

## Core Tools
Ragas, Braintrust, LangSmith, promptfoo, Hugging Face eval pipelines

## Salary (US, 2026)
- The most undervalued title on job boards: $160K–$400K+

## Who It Suits
People who love measurement and rigor — the someone-whose-full-time-job-is-knowing-if-the-AI-is-good role. Companies hiring every AI feature eventually need proof it works, and supply of this skill is far below demand.
