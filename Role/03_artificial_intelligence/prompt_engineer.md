# Prompt Engineer

Designs and optimizes the prompts given to large language models to produce accurate, safe, and useful outputs.

## What They Do
- Prompt iteration and few-shot templating
- Chain-of-thought and reasoning design
- Build evals for prompt quality
- Red-teaming prompts against failure modes
- Document prompt patterns and best practices

## Key Skills
- Deep understanding of LLM behavior
- Systematic experimentation
- Python (for orchestration and evals)
- Writing and precision (surface-level + technical)

## Core Tools
LLM APIs, prompt libraries, eval harnesses (LangSmith, Braintrust)

## Salary (US, 2026)
- Glassdoor median: ~$129K (range $102K–$166K)
- Senior specialists: $270K+
- Early fame came from Anthropic's famous listing and Scale AI

## Important Trend
Declining as a standalone title — being absorbed into AI Engineer and Context Engineer roles. The underlying skill (context/prompt engineering) remains essential inside every AI engineering job.

## Who It Suits
Writers, linguists, and communications people who can combine technical prompt literacy with domain expertise.
