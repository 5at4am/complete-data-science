# AI Research Scientist

Advances the state of the art — designs new algorithms, architectures, and approaches, and publishes findings. The most academic role on an AI team.

## What They Do
- Read and extend academic literature
- Design novel algorithms and model architectures
- Run large-scale experiments and ablations
- Publish papers at top venues (NeurIPS, ICML, ICLR, ACL, CVPR)
- Work on long-term problems, not immediate product needs

## Key Skills
- Deep mathematics: linear algebra, probability, optimization, statistics
- PyTorch, JAX, TensorFlow
- Experimental rigor and scholarly writing
- Focused specialization (NLP, CV, RL, reasoning, scaling, etc.)

## Core Tools
PyTorch, JAX, Hugging Face, GPU clusters

## Career Growth
- Research Scientist → Senior → Principal → Distinguished / Fellow
- Frontier labs: OpenAI, Google DeepMind, Meta FAIR, Anthropic, Microsoft Research

## Salary (US, 2026)
- Total comp routinely exceeds $500K at top labs
- Highest compensation in the entire field

## Who It Suits
PhD-track researchers who want to push what's possible rather than ship what's asked. Smallest market of all roles — requires a doctorate for frontier labs.
