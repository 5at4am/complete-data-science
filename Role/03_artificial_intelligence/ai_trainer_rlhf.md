# AI Trainer / RLHF Annotator

The human-in-the-loop role that makes AI systems better. Evaluates model outputs, labels training data, writes and ranks prompts, and provides the feedback signal that aligns models with human preferences (RLHF / Constitutional AI).

## What They Do
- Evaluate and rank model outputs for quality
- Label and curate training data (text, image, code)
- Write, rewrite, and rank prompts
- Provide preference feedback for RLHF and reward modeling
- Detect and document failure modes in model responses
- Maintain quality rubrics and annotation guidelines

## Key Skills
- Domain expertise (code, medical, legal, math, etc.)
- Writing and evaluation judgment
- Attention to detail and consistency
- Analytical reading of AI outputs
- Experience either found or built quickly with good guidelines

## Core Tools
Annotation platforms (Scale AI, Surge AI, internal labeling tools), prompt ranking UIs

## Career Growth
- AI Trainer → Senior Data Annotator → AI Quality Analyst → Evals Engineer
- Common leap: AI Trainer → AI Product Manager (with experience)

## Salary (US, 2026)
- Entry-level, varies heavily: $30K–$80K depending on specialization and locale
- Many roles are independent/contract in nature

## Who It Suits
Entry-point into AI with zero coding required. The oldest and most human AI role — every frontier model's quality depends on this pipeline. Good on-ramp for domain experts into the AI industry.
