# AI Red Teamer

Adversarially tests AI systems to find failures, jailbreaks, biases, and safety vulnerabilities before adversaries do.

## What They Do
- Attempt jailbreaks, prompt injections, and adversarial attacks
- Discover failure modes: hallucination, data poisoning, bias
- Map system exploitability and risk surface
- Write attack reports and remediation guidance
- Build automated red-team harnesses
- Partner with evals and safety engineers

## Key Skills
- Deep LLM behavior knowledge
- Prompt injection and jailbreak technique knowledge
- Security/penetration-testing mindset
- Python automation and eval harness building
- Structured, high-signal reporting

## Core Tools
LLM APIs, open models, ad-hoc attack harnesses, LangSmith/Braintrust for at-scale probing

## Career Growth
- AI Red Teamer → Senior → AI Safety / Evals leadership
- Distinct from AI Safety Engineer: Red Teamer attacks; Safety Engineer remediates.

## Salary (US, 2026)
- Tracks AI Engineer/researcher bands: $150K–$300K+

## Who It Suits
Adversarial, curious testers who enjoy breaking things on purpose. The role formalizes "find me every way this AI can go wrong."
