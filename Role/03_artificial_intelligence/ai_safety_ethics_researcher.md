# AI Safety & Ethics Researcher

Keeps AI systems fair, compliant, and safe — and asks whether something should be built at all. Works on alignment, red-teaming, bias, and governance.

## What They Do
- Research AI alignment and steering model behavior
- Red-team models for harmful or unsafe outputs
- Audit systems for bias, fairness, and discrimination
- Design safety evaluations and guardrails
- Advise on ethical deployment and governance
- Write policy and research papers

## Key Skills
- ML fundamentals (deep) + human values framing
- Interpretability and mechanistic analysis
- Red-teaming, adversarial testing
- Policy, ethics, law awareness (EU AI Act, etc.)
- Communication across technical and non-technical audiences

## Core Tools
PyTorch, interpretability tooling, evals harnesses, annotation/audit platforms

## Career Growth
- Safety Researcher → Senior → Principal; sits in both research and policy lanes
- New role categories keep emerging: Alignment Engineer, Model Behavior Engineer, AI Red Teamer

## Salary (US, 2026)
- Research-track: comparable to research scientists ($200K–$500K+)
- Ethics/policy-track: more variable

## Who It Suits
Reflective, safety-conscious researchers who care about whether AI systems are trustworthy — the role that formalizes "should this be built at all?"
