# Generative AI Engineer (GenAI Engineer)

AI Engineer scoped to generative models — text, image, audio, video. In practice nearly identical to AI Engineer but preferred branding at enterprise services and design companies.

## What They Do
- Prompt design and optimization
- RAG, fine-tuning, multimodal pipelines
- Build text/image/audio/video generation systems
- Safety filters and output moderation
- Cost/latency management for generation workloads

## Key Skills
- LLM/generative model APIs and open models
- Multimodal pipelines (vision, audio, video gen)
- Prompt/context engineering
- Fine-tuning and model routing
- Evaluation of generated outputs

## Core Tools
OpenAI/Anthropic/Google APIs, open models (Llama, Mistral, Stable Diffusion), LangChain, vector DBs

## Career Growth
- GenAI Engineer → Senior → Staff → GenAI Architect
- Heavy hiring at Accenture, Deloitte, Capgemini, Adobe, Canva

## Salary (US, 2026)
- Base: $175K–$250K
- Total comp: ~$220K–$350K

## Note
Peaked as a distinct title in late 2024; now consolidating into the plain "AI Engineer" title. Still the label most enterprise consulting firms use.
