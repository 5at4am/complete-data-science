# AI Systems Engineer

Builds and operates the end-to-end AI application stack — beyond individual model features to the whole system: orchestration, integration, scaling, and reliability.

## What They Do
- Architect end-to-end AI application systems
- Integrate LLMs, retrieval, orchestration, and data services
- Design for scale, fault tolerance, and reliability
- Handle multi-model routing and fallback logic
- Own observability across AI subsystems
- Engineer guardrails and safety filters into the system

## Key Skills
- Systems architecture and distributed systems
- Python/Go/TypeScript backend engineering
- LLM orchestration + vector infrastructure
- SRE practices: SLIs/SLOs, incident response, chaos testing
- Cloud platform architecture

## Core Tools
Kubernetes, Docker, Terraform, LLM APIs, vector DBs, observability stack (Prometheus, Grafana, OpenTelemetry)

## Career Growth
- AI Systems Engineer → Senior → Staff → AI Architect / AI Platform Lead

## Salary (US, 2026)
- $160K–$320K+ depending on tier

## Who It Suits
Product/backend engineers who think in whole systems — the person who makes sure the AI product survives real users, real traffic, and real failures.
