# AI / Data Science / ML Roles

A curated, research-backed collection of career roles in **Data Science**, **Machine Learning**, and **Artificial Intelligence** (2026 edition). Sourced from live job-marketing research (LinkedIn, Levels.fyi, Lightcast, Robert Half, and industry guides).

## The Big Picture

The data/AI field is no longer one job — it is a family of specialized roles spread across four tracks:

| Track | Focus | Typical Roles |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Data Science** | Insights, statistics, business decisions | Data Analyst, Data Scientist, Decision Scientist, BI Analyst |
| **Machine Learning** | Building, training, and shipping models | ML Engineer, Deep Learning Specialist, MLOps Engineer, Applied Scientist |
| **Artificial Intelligence** | LLMs, agents, generative products | AI Engineer, GenAI Engineer, Prompt Engineer, Agent Engineer |
| **Leadership & Infrastructure** | Product, platform, governance, strategy | AI Product Manager, AI Architect, AI Governance Manager, Chief AI Officer |

## The Three Career Lanes (Data Scientist split)

Research on 2026 hiring shows the old "Data Scientist" title has split into three lanes:

1. **Product / Decision Science** — experiments, causal inference, metrics, closest to the business.
2. **Model / Production Science** — churn scoring, forecasting, fraud, model monitoring.
3. **Research Science** — novel methods, published papers, frontier labs (PhD track).

## How to Choose

| You enjoy... | Go toward... |
| --- | --- |
| Writing code and shipping systems | ML Engineer, AI Engineer, Data Engineer |
| Math, statistics, experimentation | Data Scientist, Research Scientist |
| Business communication & strategy | Data Analyst, Decision Scientist, AI PM |
| Language/visual data | NLP/LLM Engineer, Computer Vision Engineer |
| Keeping systems reliable at scale | MLOps, AI Infrastructure, Platform Engineer |
| Policy, risk, compliance | AI Governance, AI Safety & Ethics |
