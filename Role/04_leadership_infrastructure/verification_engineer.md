# Verification Engineer (Emerging)

The emerging QA + domain-expert hybrid for AI-built systems. Runs the system end-to-end, hunts broken edge cases, and verifies the AI actually built the right thing for the business — not just something that runs.

## What They Do
- Run AI-built systems end-to-end looking for broken edge cases
- Verify business logic: regulatory cases, currency conversions, billing exceptions
- Maintain a domain knowledge base that powers verification
- Sign off on anything touching money, safety, or compliance
- Act as the human approval gate for high-risk AI output
- Feed findings back into the AI pipeline to improve it

## Key Skills
- QA/test engineering + deep domain expertise (finance, healthcare, legal, industrial)
- Understanding of where and how AI systems fail
- Keeping a living, high-quality domain knowledge base
- Clear risk communication to stakeholders
- Automation for test execution + manual judgment for what to check

## Core Tools
Test automation frameworks (Playwright/Pytest), AI eval harnesses, documentation/knowledge tooling

## Career Growth
- Verification Engineer → Principal → domain QA leadership
- The knowledge base they maintain is effectively "the product"

## Why It Matters
For low-risk code, AI will self-check or auto-accept. For anything that touches money, safety, or compliance, a human Verification Engineer signs off. Predicted to be one of the two dominant emerging titles of 2026-2027 (alongside AI Delivery Engineer).

## Who It Suits
QA engineers who also care about the business — the person who can name the one edge case that would be catastrophic and prove the system handles it.
