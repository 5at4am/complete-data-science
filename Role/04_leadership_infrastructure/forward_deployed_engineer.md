# Forward-Deployed Engineer (FDE)

Sits with the customer rather than at headquarters. Helps clients who want to use AI but lack the technical staff to wire it up. Borrowed from Palantir's model — now exploding across AI labs.

## What They Do
- Embed with enterprise customers and deploy AI at their sites
- Understand customer business problems deeply
- Wire up RAG pilots, agents, and custom AI features
- Write production code in customer environments
- Iterate on-site until the AI delivers real business value
- Feedback customer needs to product/research teams

## Key Skills
- Strong full-stack + AI engineering
- Consulting instincts and fast turnaround
- Systems design in unfamiliar environments
- Communication with non-technical executives
- Resilience under customer pressure

## Core Tools
Anything the customer runs — LLM APIs, RAG stacks, cloud platforms, internal systems

## Career Growth
- FDE → Senior FDE → Solutions Architect → Account/Delivery leadership
- Variants: Forward-Deployed AI Engineer, AI Solutions Engineer, AI Delivery Engineer

## Salary (US, 2026)
- Palantir average: ~$238K TC
- OpenAI/Anthropic bands: $350K–$550K+

## Reality Check
FT reported FDE postings jumped 800%+ (Jan–Sep 2025), but developers often quit — it's high-pressure, customer-facing consulting with a nicer title. Hot and potentially oversold; great money, tough job.
