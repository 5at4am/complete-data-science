# AI Architect

A step above AI Engineer in scope. Decides how AI systems fit together across a company: which models go where, how data flows between them, and how the pieces hold up as technology shifts underneath.

## What They Do
- Design enterprise-wide AI system architecture
- Choose models, orchestration, and retrieval across the stack
- Define data flows and integration patterns
- Set architecture standards and guardrails
- Evaluate build-vs-buy and model vendor strategy
- Define long-term technical roadmap for AI

## Key Skills
- Deep AI/LLM engineering experience
- Systems architecture and distributed systems
- Cloud architecture (AWS/Azure/GCP)
- Cost, latency, quality trade-off engineering
- Technical leadership and communication

## Core Tools
Cloud platforms, LLM APIs, vector DBs, orchestration frameworks, IaC tooling

## Career Growth
- AI Engineer → Senior → Staff → AI Architect → AI Technical Lead / CAIO
- Box created this role explicitly as "AI architect"

## Salary (US, 2026)
- Typically $200K–$400K+ depending on company and tier

## Who It Suits
Senior engineers who have shipped multiple AI systems and want to own the architecture, not just individual features.
