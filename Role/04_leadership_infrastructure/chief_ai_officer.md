# Chief AI Officer (CAIO)

The newest C-suite title and one of the fastest-growing executive roles. Owns the company's AI strategy end to end: which problems to solve with AI, build-vs-buy decisions, AI ethics and governance, vendor evaluation, and measuring AI ROI.

## What They Do
- Set enterprise AI strategy and vision
- Build and lead the AI team (science, engineering, product)
- Own build-vs-buy and AI vendor decisions
- Define, monitor, and report AI ROI
- Drive AI governance, ethics, and compliance
- Champion AI adoption across the organization
- Report to the CEO alongside CTO/CPO

## Key Skills
- Executive leadership and organizational change
- Deep AI/ML domain credibility (often engineering/research background)
- Business strategy and ROI financials
- Risk, governance, and regulatory ownership
- Communication with board and investors

## Core Tools
Strategic roadmaps, AI spend/P&L, vendor management, governance frameworks

## Career Growth
- Head of AI / VP AI → Chief AI Officer
- Can rise from AI Architect, Head of Data Science, or AI Platform Lead

## Salary (US, 2026)
- $352K–$1M+ total comp
- One of the fastest-growing C-suite seats

## Who It Suits
Senior leaders who can combine technical AI judgment with business accountability — the person who answers for what AI actually returns to the company.
