# AI Platform Engineer

Builds and manages the internal platform that lets every other engineer build AI faster — ML/LLM platform teams. Runs the factory: the tooling, access, and standards that keep AI development from fragmenting.

## What They Do
- Build internal self-serve AI/ML platforms
- Model registry, experiment tracking, and model lifecycle tooling
- Feature stores and reusable prompt/context infrastructure
- Standardize model deployment, evals, and guardrails
- Govern access and cost across teams
- Onboard data scientists/AI engineers with curated tooling
- CI/CD for ML and LLM pipelines

## Key Skills
- MLflow, Kubeflow, Feast, W&B
- Kubernetes, Docker, Terraform
- Backend platform engineering (APIs, SDKs)
- Developer experience (DX) for internal users
- Cost, security, and governance plumbing

## Core Tools
MLflow, Kubeflow, Feast, LangSmith, Kubernetes, Terraform, JupyterHub

## Career Growth
- AI Platform Engineer → Senior → Staff → Platform Lead / Head of ML Platform

## Salary (US, 2026)
- $140K–$240K+ tier 1; senior platform leads higher

## Difference vs AI Infra
AI Infrastructure Engineer runs the GPU cluster and serving stack. AI Platform Engineer builds the developer-facing platform on top of it. Dev-facing = Platform; compute/serving = Infra.
