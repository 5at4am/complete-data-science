# AI Governance Manager

Ensures AI systems comply with regulations (EU AI Act, state-level AI laws, sector-specific rules), creates ethics frameworks, conducts risk assessments, and manages the audit trail. Sits at the intersection of legal, compliance, and technology.

## What They Do
- Implement AI risk and compliance frameworks
- Conduct AI risk assessments and impact evaluations
- Create AI ethics policies and usage guidelines
- Maintain audit trails and model documentation
- Manage AI vendor and procurement reviews
- Oversee data privacy for AI systems
- Report AI readiness to leadership/boards

## Key Skills
- Regulatory literacy: EU AI Act, local AI laws, sector rules
- Risk management frameworks (NIST AI RMF, ISO 42001)
- Data privacy (GDPR/CCPA) and security basics
- Enough ML understanding to judge systemic risk
- Legal/compliance writing and stakeholder communication

## Core Tools
Governance/compliance platforms, risk frameworks, audit tooling, model documentation standards

## Career Growth
- AI Governance Analyst → Manager → Director → Chief AI Officer / Chief Risk Officer track
- Also: AI Risk Lead, AI Compliance Officer, AI Ethics Lead

## Salary (US, 2026)
- $120K–$220K+ depending on seniority and company size

## Who It Suits
Legal/compliance people who can learn ML literacy — the role every AI-adopting company now needs whether or not it has the title.
