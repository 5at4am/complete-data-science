# AI Infrastructure Engineer

Runs the GPU cluster and serving stack. Keeps AI compute available, fast, and affordable. "The hottest job in AI right now — compute is the bottleneck."

## What They Do
- Provision and manage GPU clusters (CUDA, Kubernetes, Slurm)
- Build model serving infrastructure (vLLM, Triton, Ray)
- Optimize inference/ training cost and utilization
- Multi-cloud / data-center GPU planning
- Networking, storage, and SRE for AI workloads
- Capacity planning for model demand

## Key Skills
- Kubernetes, Docker, container orchestration
- CUDA, GPU operators (Nvidia GPU Operator, k8s device plugins)
- vLLM, Triton Inference Server, TensorRT
- Terraform/cloud IaC, SRE practices
- Distributed systems and networking

## Core Tools
Kubernetes, vLLM, Triton, Docker, Terraform, AWS/GCP/Azure, Slurm, Ray

## Career Growth
- AI Infra Engineer → Senior → Staff → Principal / AI Platform Lead

## Salary (US, 2026)
- $220K–$600K+ (hot market, compute is the bottleneck)

## Who It Suits
Systems/SRE/DevOps engineers who want to own GPU infrastructure and the serving stack that every AI product depends on.
