# AI Delivery Engineer (Emerging)

Predicted to be the most common engineering title in companies under 200 people by 2027. Owns the full arc from vague business intent to a shipped outcome, using AI as the primary production tool.

## What They Do
- Turn vague business requests into working AI-powered products
- Decide what technology to reach for (adapting automation to risk)
- Guide AI through generation with varying levels of automation
- Review output, iterate, and ship
- Absorb the roles of engineer + PM + tech lead at small companies

## Key Skills
- Full-stack + AI engineering fundamentals
- Product thinking and requirements distillation
- Judgment about when to let AI act autonomously vs. step in
- Rapid iteration and delivery discipline
- Verification of business logic in AI output

## Core Tools
LLM code agents, standard dev stack, eval/QA tooling, whatever ships the outcome

## Career Growth
- Still forming; the emergent senior path is essentially "delivery lead for AI-shaped work"

## Why It Matters
At 80% of the market the work is the same: someone must own the outcome — not just orchestrate — and verify the result is right. Analysts predict this title consolidates engineer/PM/tech-lead responsibilities into one accountability-bearing role.

## Who It Suits
Versatile builders who prefer end-to-end ownership over deep specialization.
