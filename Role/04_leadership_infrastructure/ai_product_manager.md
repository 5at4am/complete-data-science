# AI Product Manager

Defines what AI products should do and why. Translates business problems into ML problem statements, prioritizes the model roadmap, sets success metrics, and coordinates scientists, engineers, and business stakeholders.

## What They Do
- Define AI product vision and strategy
- Translate business problems into ML/AI problem statements
- Own the cost/quality/latency triangle (AI spend)
- Prioritize features and the model roadmap
- Set success metrics and evaluation criteria
- Assess build-vs-buy for models and AI vendors
- Communicate trade-offs between accuracy and latency

## Key Skills
- Product sense and user experience
- Enough ML literacy to make informed trade-offs (not full code depth)
- Business strategy, roadmap planning, stakeholder management
- Experimentation and metrics mindset
- Vendor and cost evaluation

## Core Tools
Roadmapping tools, analytics dashboards, metric definitions, experimentation platforms

## Career Growth
- AI PM → Senior AI PM → Director of AI Product → Head of AI/CPO track

## Salary (US, 2026)
- $140K–$220K (MBA or BS in CS typical)
- $180K–$260K+ at senior/top-tier

## Who It Suits
Product managers who understand model capabilities and limitations. AI projects fail more often from poor problem definition than from poor model performance — this role keeps teams focused on business value.
