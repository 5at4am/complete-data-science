# Decision Scientist / Product Data Scientist

Lives closest to product and business. Runs experiments, sizes opportunities, builds the metrics teams argue about — the deliverable is a recommendation, not just a model.

## What They Do
- Design and run A/B experiments, uplift and switchback tests
- Causal inference on observational data
- Build metric definitions and opportunity sizing
- Advise product teams on what a launch actually did

## Key Skills
- Heavy SQL
- Causal inference libraries: DoWhy, EconML
- Experimentation platforms: Eppo, Statsig, Optimizely
- Statistical rigor + business judgment
- Light, deliberately simple modeling

## Why It's GenAI-Resistant
The role is fundamentally about judgment — deciding which question is worth answering and knowing when the data can't support the claim. Routine analysis is automated; this judgment is not.

## Core Tools
SQL, Python, Eppo, Statsig, DoWhy, EconML

## Salary (US, 2026)
- Entry: $95K–$120K
- Senior: $160K–$210K

## Career Path
Product lane promotes the fastest because work lands in decisions people remember. Jump to Head of Analytics or Chief Data Officer is short.
