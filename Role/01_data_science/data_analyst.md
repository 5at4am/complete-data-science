# Data Analyst

The most accessible entry point into the data field. Analyzes data to produce insights, reports, and dashboards that support business decision-making.

## What They Do
- Collect, clean, and process data from multiple sources
- Identify trends, patterns, and anomalies
- Build dashboards and reports using SQL and BI tools
- Communicate findings to non-technical stakeholders

## Key Skills
- SQL (essential), Excel
- Data visualization: Tableau, Power BI, Looker, matplotlib/plotly
- Analytical thinking and problem solving
- Python/Pandas (increasingly required)
- Storytelling with data

## Core Tools
SQL, Excel, Tableau, Power BI, Looker, Pandas

## Career Growth
- Senior Analyst → BI Analyst → Data Scientist or Data Engineer
- Analytics Manager / Head of Analytics

## Salary (US, 2026)
- Entry: $60K–$85K
- Mid: $85K–$120K
- Senior: $120K–$155K

## Who It Suits
People who enjoy investigation and analysis, are naturally curious about data, and can clearly communicate findings to business audiences.
