# Business Intelligence (BI) Analyst

Turns data into strategic insights through dashboards and KPIs. The bridge between technical data teams and business stakeholders.

## What They Do
- Analyze data and build exec-facing dashboards and reports
- Track KPIs and business metrics
- Provide insights that improve business performance
- Data-driven storytelling for leadership

## Key Skills
- SQL
- BI tools: Tableau, Power BI, Looker
- Data visualization and business understanding
- Strong stakeholder communication

## Core Tools
SQL, Tableau, Power BI, Looker, Excel

## Career Growth
- BI Analyst → Senior Analyst → BI Developer → Analytics Manager → Head of Analytics

## Salary (US, 2026)
- Entry: $55K–$80K
- Mid: $80K–$110K
- Senior: $110K–$140K

## Who It Suits
Heavy business domain knowledge fans who prefer strategic, stakeholder-facing work over deep modeling.
