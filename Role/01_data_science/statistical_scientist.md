# Statistical Scientist

Applies rigorous statistical methods to answer complex questions with confidence. Critical in fields where accuracy and validity matter — pharma, healthcare, clinical trials, scientific research.

## What They Do
- Design experiments (clinical trials, DOE)
- Analyze clinical and operational data
- Survival analysis, biostatistics, hypothesis testing
- Explain what results do (or don't) prove

## Key Skills
- Advanced statistics: inference, survival analysis, mixed models
- R, SAS, SPSS as much as Python
- Experimental design
- Scientific writing and rigor

## Core Tools
R, Python, SAS, SPSS, SQL, Stan, JAGS

## Career Growth
- Statistical Scientist → Senior → Principal Biostatistician → Director of Biostatistics

## Who It Suits
Math/stats/epidemiology backgrounds. Strong candidates often come from actuarial, epidemiological, or clinical data work and are worth more on day one when they bring the domain with them.
