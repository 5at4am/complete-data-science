# Applied Scientist

Sits between Research Scientists and ML Engineers — takes recent research and figures out how to apply it to real product problems. Common at Amazon, Meta, Anthropic, Microsoft.

## What They Do
- Prototype new research approaches against product problems
- Run rigorous experiments and evaluations
- Bridge academic literature to production code
- Build novel models that solve practical challenges
- Partner with engineers to ship successful experiments

## Key Skills
- Deep ML theory + strong engineering
- PyTorch/TensorFlow, Python
- Experimentation and rigorous evaluation
- Publication record or strong Kaggle/competition signal
- Proven production ML

## Core Tools
PyTorch, JAX, Hugging Face, MLflow

## Career Growth
- Applied Scientist → Senior → Principal Applied Scientist
- Exits: Research Scientist (more research) or ML Engineer (more engineering)

## Salary (US, 2026)
- Typical base: $190K–$245K
- Total comp at top labs: $300K–$500K+

## Who It Suits
MS/PhD candidates with strong research signal who still want to see their work ship in real products.
