# Deep Learning Specialist

Concentrates on neural network architectures — deep learning research and development, often within NLP or computer vision domains.

## What They Do
- Design and train deep neural network architectures
- Research and adapt modern architectures (transformers, GANs, diffusion, RNNs)
- Performance optimization: quantization, pruning, distillation
- Distributed and GPU-accelerated training

## Key Skills
- Deep learning theory: backpropagation, optimization, regularization
- PyTorch, JAX; CUDA/GPU programming
- Transformers, attention mechanisms
- Distributed training (DDP, FSDP, DeepSpeed)
- Linear algebra, calculus, probability

## Core Tools
PyTorch, JAX, TensorFlow, CUDA, DeepSpeed, Hugging Face

## Career Growth
- Deep Learning Engineer → Senior → Staff → Research-track exits

## Who It Suits
People who love the math and architectures behind neural networks and want to push model capability forward, typically with strong academic foundations.
