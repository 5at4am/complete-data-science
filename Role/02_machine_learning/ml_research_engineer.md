# ML Research Engineer

Converts research ideas into practical, scalable systems. The dominant engineering title inside frontier AI labs — research plus engineering in one role.

## What They Do
- Implement novel algorithms and models from papers
- Build distributed training + inference infrastructure
- Prototype models, make them reliable and scalable
- Run large-scale experiments and benchmark studies
- Bridge research and production engineering

## Key Skills
- Strong math + systems engineering
- PyTorch, JAX, CUDA, distributed training
- High-performance computing and GPU clusters
- Production engineering: serving, scaling, reliability

## Core Tools
PyTorch, JAX, CUDA, DeepSpeed, Ray, vLLM

## Career Growth
- ML Research Engineer → Senior → Staff → Technical Lead
- The dominant role at OpenAI, Anthropic, DeepMind

## Salary (US, 2026)
- Frontier labs: $350K–$1.4M+ total comp
- Typical range: $250K–$500K+

## Who It Suits
People who refuse to choose between research and engineering — they want to invent things AND make them run at scale. PhD common but not always required.
