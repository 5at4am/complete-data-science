# LLMOps Engineer

Applies the MLOps discipline to large language models specifically. Keeps LLM systems and prompts from breaking in production — monitoring, evaluation, drift, cost, and reliability for generative workloads.

## What They Do
- Deploy and serve LLMs (vLLM, TGI, quantized models)
- Monitor generation quality, latency, and cost in production
- Track prompt/behavior drift over time
- Build evaluation and regression test pipelines for LLMs
- Manage guardrails, safety filters, and abuse resistance
- Operate RAG infrastructure and vector stores

## Key Skills
- vLLM, TGI, Hugging Face inference stacks
- Kubernetes, Docker, observability stack
- Eval harnesses: Ragas, Braintrust, LangSmith
- Token/quota cost optimization
- Guardrails frameworks and prompt monitoring

## Core Tools
vLLM, Kubernetes, MLflow, LangSmith, Braintrust, vector DBs, cloud platforms

## Career Growth
- LLMOps Engineer → Senior → ML Platform / AI Reliability roles

## Salary (US, 2026)
- $161K–$240K (underpaid relative to criticality, per industry observers)

## Who It Suits
DevOps/MLOps engineers who want to specialize in the operational side of generative AI — the people who keep prompts and models from breaking after launch.
