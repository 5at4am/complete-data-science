# TaxKraft — Frequently Asked Questions

## Q: Is TaxKraft a government agency?
TaxKraft is a private professional services firm (CA-led). It files and registers through
official government portals (GST portal, income-tax portal, MCA, etc.). It is not a
government body. VERIFY: keep wording aligned with the website's disclaimer if published.

## Q: Does TaxKraft file GST returns?
Yes — TaxKraft offers GST monthly, quarterly, and annual return filing, plus e-commerce GST
filing. See `services_gst.md`.

## Q: Does TaxKraft file income tax returns?
Yes — TaxKraft offers ITR filing for all major categories (ITR-1 through ITR-7 as applicable)
and TDS return filing. See `services_income_tax.md`.

## Q: Can TaxKraft register my company?
Yes — TaxKraft registers Private Limited, Public Limited, LLP, OPC, Partnership, Section 8, and
Sole Proprietorship entities, and supports Startup India registration. See
`services_company_registration.md`.

## Q: Will anyone actually talk to me, or is it all software?
TaxKraft advertises "human support, not just software" — expert CAs are involved in every
engagement, with year-round consultation for clients. See `pricing_and_process.md`.

## Q: Is TaxKraft available in Hindi?
TaxKraft's public contact data lists English and Hindi as supported languages.

## Q: Do I need to visit the office?
TaxKraft's services are delivered online across India; the office is in Noida, Uttar Pradesh.
For documents requiring original signatures (e.g., DSC-linked filings), experts will guide you
on the exact process. VERIFY: per-service document requirements from live pages.

## Q: What if I got a tax or GST notice?
TaxKraft offers GST notices & litigation support and income-tax notice response drafting.
Contact support to share the notice. See `services_gst.md` and `services_income_tax.md`.
