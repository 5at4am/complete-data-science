# TaxKraft — Registrations & Licenses

TaxKraft handles registrations and licenses beyond company incorporation. Service list from
the public sitemap:

1. **Trademark Registration** — `/service/trademark-registration`. Trademark application and
   related support (objections/assignment as applicable per service pages).
2. **MSME Registration** — `/service/msme-registration`. MSME / Udyam-registration-adjacent
   enterprise registration support.
3. **Udyam Registration** — `/service/udyam-registration`. Udyam (MSME) registration for micro,
   small and medium enterprises.
4. **FSSAI Registration** — `/service/fssai-registration`. Food business operator licence /
   registration.
5. **IEC Registration** — `/service/iec-registration`. Import-Export Code for foreign trade.
6. **IEC Modification** — `/service/iec-modification`. Changes to an existing IEC.
7. **ISO Registration** — `/service/iso-registration`. ISO certification assistance.
8. **GEM Registration** — `/service/gem-registration`. Government e-Marketplace registration.
9. **Shop and Establishment Registration** — `/service/shop-and-establishment-registration`.
10. **PF Registration** — `/service/pf-registration`. Provident fund code registration.
11. **ESI Registration** — `/service/esi-registration`. Employees' State Insurance registration.
12. **PAN Registration** — `/service/pan-registration`. Permanent Account Number application.
13. **TAN Registration** — `/service/tan-registration`. Tax deduction/collection account number.
14. **Digital Signature Certificate (DSC)** — `/service/digital-signature-certificate`.
15. **BIS Certification** — `/service/bis-certification`. Bureau of Indian Standards
    certification support.
16. **ISI Mark Registration** — `/service/isi-mark-registration`. ISI marking for products.
17. **LEI Registration** — `/service/lei-registration`. Legal Entity Identifier for financial
    transactions.

VERIFY: document checklists and turnaround expectations per service on the live pages.

## For assistant use

If the user names any of the above licenses/registrations, confirm TaxKraft offers it and
point to a human expert for documents and fees. Deflect eligibility questions about
government schemes to the experts.
