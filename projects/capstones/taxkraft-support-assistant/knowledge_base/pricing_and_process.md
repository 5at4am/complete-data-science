# TaxKraft — Pricing, Process & Approach

## How TaxKraft describes its approach

- **Zero hidden fees** — published stance on transparent pricing.
- **Human support, not just software** — every engagement includes CA/expert assistance, not
  an automated-only service.
- **Tech-enabled processes and timely reminders** — automated tracking plus notifications so
  deadlines are not missed.
- **Year-round consultation included** — the engagement covers ongoing guidance, not a
  one-time filing.
- Built for **individuals, startups, and SMEs** across India.

## Typical engagement flow (as implied by TaxKraft's published process)

1. Reach out via phone/WhatsApp/email or the website contact/order flow.
2. Share the documents requested by the expert for the specific service.
3. TaxKraft experts complete the registration/filing/compliance.
4. The client receives confirmation, copies, and reminders for future deadlines.
