# TaxKraft — GST Services

TaxKraft provides end-to-end GST services: registration, return filing (monthly, quarterly,
annual), e-commerce GST support, notice handling and litigation support, modification and
cancellation, and LUT registration for exporters.

## Services offered (from the public sitemap)

1. **GST Registration** — `/service/gst-registration`. New GSTIN enrolment under the GST Act.
2. **GST Monthly Return** — `/service/gst-monthly-return`. GSTR-1 / GSTR-3B monthly filing support.
3. **GST Quarterly Return** — `/service/gst-quarterly-return`. QRMP quarterly filing support.
4. **GST Annual Return** — `/service/gst-annual-return`. GSTR-9 / GSTR-9C annual filing.
5. **GST Filing for E-commerce** — `/service/gst-filing-for-e-commerce`. Compliance for
   e-commerce operators / sellers.
6. **GST Registration for E-commerce Sellers** — `/service/gst-registration-for-e-commerce-sellers`.
7. **GST Modification** — `/service/gst-modification`. Changes to GST registration details.
8. **GST Cancellation** — `/service/gst-cancellation`. Surrendering/cancelling a GSTIN.
9. **GST Cancellation Return** — `/service/gst-cancellation-return`. Final return filing on
   cancellation (GSTR-10 support).
10. **GST Notices and Litigation** — `/service/gst-notices-and-litigation`. Response and
    representation support for GST notices / demands.
11. **Letter of Undertaking (LUT) Registration** — `/service/letter-of-undertaking-lut-registration`.
    For exporters who export without payment of IGST.

## General, safe grounding (public-domain, low-risk)

- GST is a consumption-based goods-and-services tax in India; businesses above the applicable
  threshold are required to register and file returns.
- Monthly vs. quarterly filing depends on turnover and scheme (e.g., QRMP for eligible
  taxpayers); annual returns depend on turnover (e.g., GSTR-9C above a threshold).
- Nil filings are still required in months with no transactions.
