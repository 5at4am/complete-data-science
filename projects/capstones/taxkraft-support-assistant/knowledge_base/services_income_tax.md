# TaxKraft — Income Tax Services

TaxKraft offers income-tax return (ITR) filing for all common categories plus TDS/TCS,
notices/assessments, and NGO/FCRA-related registrations.

## ITR filing services (from the public sitemap)

1. **ITR-1 Sahaj** — `/service/itr-1-sahaj`. For salaried individuals (simplest return form).
2. **ITR-2** — `/service/itr-2`. Individuals/HUFs with capital gains or foreign assets.
3. **ITR-3** — `/service/itr-3`. Individuals/HUFs with business/professional income.
4. **ITR-4 Sugam** — `/service/itr-4`. For presumptive-income businesses/professions.
5. **ITR-5** — `/service/itr-5`. Firms, LLPs, AOPs/BOIs.
6. **ITR-6** — `/service/itr-6`. Companies other than those claiming Section 11 exemption.
7. **ITR-7** — `/service/itr-7`. Persons including companies required to furnish returns under
   Sections 139(4A), 139(4B), 139(4C) or 139(4D).

Verification note: the association of each ITR form number with its typical filer category
above is standard public tax-education content; VERIFY the exact scope descriptions on each
TaxKraft service page before quoting them.

## TDS / TCS

8. **TDS Return Filing** — `/service/tds-return-filing`. Quarterly TDS/TCS return filing and
   corrections with professional support.

## Notices & assessments

9. **Income Tax Assessments** — `/service/income-tax-assessments`. Support during tax
   assessments (scrutiny, e-assessment, etc.).
10. **Income Tax Notice** — `/service/income-tax-notice`. Drafting responses to income-tax
    notices.

## Registration / exemption support

11. **12A & 80G Registration** — `/service/12a-and-80g-registration`. Charitable/NGO trust
    registrations and 80G donation exemption.
12. **80-IAC Tax Exemption** — `/service/80-iac-tax-exemption`. Startups seeking 80-IAC
    tax-holiday benefits.
13. **FCRA Registration** — `/service/fcra-registration`. Foreign Contribution Regulation Act
    registration (and renewal support as applicable).
14. **Forms 15CA / 15CB** — `/service/forms-15ca-15cb`. Certificates/forms for remittances
    to non-residents.

## For assistant use

Match the user to the right ITR form *as a categorization of TaxKraft's services*, not as
personal legal advice. When the user describes a personal situation and asks which form to
file or how much tax they owe, deflect to TaxKraft's experts. Never compute taxes.
