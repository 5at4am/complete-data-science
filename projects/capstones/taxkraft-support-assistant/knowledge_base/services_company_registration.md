# TaxKraft — Company Registration Services

TaxKraft helps register and incorporate businesses of all common legal forms in India.
The public sitemap lists the following company-registration services:

## Services offered

1. **Private Limited Company** — incorporation of a private limited company
   (`/service/private-limited-company`). VERIFY: document list and timeline on the service page.
2. **Public Limited Company** — incorporation of a public limited company (`/service/public-limited-company`).
3. **Limited Liability Partnership (LLP)** — LLP incorporation (`/service/limited-liability-partnership`).
4. **One Person Company (OPC)** — single-member company incorporation (`/service/one-person-company`).
5. **Partnership Firm** — partnership firm registration (`/service/partnership-firm`).
6. **Section 8 Company** — not-for-profit / charitable company registration (`/service/section-8-company`).
7. **Sole Proprietorship** — sole proprietorship setup (`/service/sole-proprietorship`).
8. **Startup India Registration** — Startup India recognition/registration support
   (`/service/startup-india-registration`).

## What a typical registration involves (as TaxKraft describes its process)

- Professional guidance on choosing the right entity type for the client's goals.
- Document collection and verification (identity, address, business proofs).
- Handling of government-portal paperwork (name reservation, DIN/DSC, incorporation documents).
- Post-registration follow-ups such as PAN, TAN, GST and bank-account setup support.
