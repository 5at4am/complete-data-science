# TaxKraft — Company Overview

## Who is TaxKraft

TaxKraft is a CA, tax, GST and business-registration services firm in India. The company
positions itself as "Your Business Growth Partner" with the tagline "Beyond Compliance.
Towards Growth." It helps individuals, startups, SMEs, and growing enterprises with GST,
Income Tax, accounting, company registration, and ROC compliance — all through qualified
Chartered Accountants (CAs) and tax experts.

According to TaxKraft's public materials, the business started as a small initiative to help
individuals with annual income-tax return (ITR) filing and evolved into a comprehensive
platform covering GST registration and filing, income tax support, accounting, registrations,
compliance management, and advisory for businesses across India. VERIFY: homepage states
"15,000+ businesses served."

## What TaxKraft helps with (high-level)

- GST & tax compliance — GST registration, monthly/quarterly returns, ITC reconciliation,
  GST notice support, ITR filing, TDS/TCS return filing and tax planning.
- Business registration — Private Limited, LLP, One Person Company, Partnership, Section 8,
  Startup India registration, plus PAN / TAN / GST / bank account setup support.
- Accounting & ROC compliance — monthly bookkeeping, financial reports & MIS, ROC filings and
  corporate compliance, payroll / PF / ESI support.
- Advisory & growth — tax-saving strategies, cashflow and forecasting, legal documentation,
  and business advisory (CFO services, project reports, funding support).

## Why businesses choose TaxKraft (as published)

- Zero hidden fees
- Human support, not just software
- Tech-enabled processes and timely reminders
- Year-round consultation
- Built for individuals, startups and SMEs
