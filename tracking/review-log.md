# Review Log

> **Track your review sessions to fight forgetting.**
> Understanding a notebook once is not mastery — review is essential.

---

## How to Use

For every review session, add an entry:

```text
### Review #N

- **Date:** YYYY-MM-DD
- **Units reviewed:** e.g., 05.2, 05.3
- **Method:** Closed-book recall / explain-back / rebuild from scratch
- **Score:** How well did I do? (1-5)
- **Weak areas:** What did I forget?
- **Action items:** What will I review again?
- **Next review date:** YYYY-MM-DD
```

---

## Review Schedule

### Immediate Recall
- At the end of every unit (in the notebook)

### Delayed Recall
- Review notebooks after several units

### Cumulative Review
- Phase review notebooks
- Later notebooks reuse earlier concepts
